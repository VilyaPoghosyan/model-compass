Files

CSV opens in any spreadsheet; the two JSON files are what the site itself loads. Everything is regenerated together on every export, so the files always agree with each other and with the pages.

  • prompts.csv

    80 rows, 15 KB

    One row per prompt: the request, task, required text.

    Download

  • models.csv

    12 rows, 2 KB

    One row per model: maker, tier, credits, settings, link.

    Download

  • scores.csv

    960 rows, 163 KB

    One row per prompt and model: the three automatic scores and the mean judge rank.

    Download

  • ratings.csv

    96 rows, 8 KB

    One row per task and model: Plackett–Luce strength, interval, rank, win probability.

    Download

  • compass.json

    80 prompts, 301 KB

    Everything the site shows in one file: models, tasks with rankings and recommendations, prompts with image paths.

    Download

  • router.json

    80 embeddings, 249 KB

    What the browser router loads: 384-number embeddings of every prompt, per-task recommendation, keyword fallback, evaluation.

Images sit at the paths named in scores.csv (1024 px WebP with the Picsart AI label). The 512 px thumbnails live under img/thumbs/ at the same path. Raw judge sheets and both full rankings per prompt are available on request (see about).

How the data was made

  1. 80 prompts in 8 tasks, fixed before generation (prompts.csv).
  2. Every prompt sent once to each of 12 models in Picsart's AI Playground, at the settings in models.csv. One image per prompt, no re-rolls.
  3. Three programs scored every image: prompt match, aesthetic score, and OCR spelling for text tasks (scores.csv).
  4. An AI judge ranked each prompt's images twice in shuffled order without model names; the mean position is judge_rank_mean.
  5. Per task, the rankings were fitted with Plackett–Luce and bootstrapped 1,000 times over prompts (ratings.csv).
  6. Prompts were embedded with a small sentence model for the browser router (router.json).

The full method, with every limit we know of, is on the method page.
