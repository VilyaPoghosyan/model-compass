How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 anime and illustration prompts written like real users write them, sent to 12 models you can pick in Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs in shuffled order without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Ratings with error bars. Plackett–Luce fit, bootstrapped over requests. CLIP, an aesthetic predictor are shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for anime and illustration?
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (OpenAI). On 10 anime and illustration requests it had a 16% chance of being ranked first by our judge, ahead of Nano Banana 2 at 16%. 12 Picsart AI models were tested the same way.
Is Nano Banana 2 as good as GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for anime and illustration?
Close. Their 95% error bars overlap (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +0.09 to +1.67, Nano Banana 2 +0.19 to +1.65 log-strength), so on 10 requests we cannot separate them with confidence; GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is still the more likely winner.
How was the best model for anime and illustration measured?
Every model got the same 10 requests. An AI judge ranked the outputs for each request twice in shuffled order without seeing model names (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.39). Rankings were fitted with a Plackett-Luce model and bootstrapped over requests for 95% error bars; CLIP similarity and an aesthetic predictor are supporting scores.
