How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 portrait and headshot prompts written like real users write them, sent to 12 models you can pick in Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs in shuffled order without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Ratings with error bars. Plackett–Luce fit, bootstrapped over requests. CLIP, an aesthetic predictor are shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for portrait and headshot?
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (OpenAI). On 10 portrait and headshot requests it had a 25% chance of being ranked first by our judge, ahead of Seedream 5.0 Pro at 15%. 12 Picsart AI models were tested the same way.
Is Seedream 5.0 Pro as good as GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for portrait and headshot?
Close. Their 95% error bars overlap (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +1.11 to +1.94, Seedream 5.0 Pro +0.15 to +1.98 log-strength), so on 10 requests we cannot separate them with confidence; GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is still the more likely winner.
How was the best model for portrait and headshot measured?
Every model got the same 10 requests. An AI judge ranked the outputs for each request twice in shuffled order without seeing model names (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.59). Rankings were fitted with a Plackett-Luce model and bootstrapped over requests for 95% error bars; CLIP similarity and an aesthetic predictor are supporting scores.
