How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 portrait and headshot prompts, written like real users write, sent to 12 models from Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Error bars. Plackett–Luce ratings bootstrapped over requests; CLIP, an aesthetic predictor shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for portraits and headshots?
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (OpenAI): 25% chance of ranking first on 10 portraits and headshots requests, ahead of Seedream 5.0 Pro at 15%, out of 12 models tested the same way.
Is Seedream 5.0 Pro as good as GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for portraits and headshots?
Close: their 95% error bars overlap (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +1.11 to +1.94, Seedream 5.0 Pro +0.15 to +1.98 log-strength), so on 10 requests GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is only the more likely winner.
How was the best model for portraits and headshots measured?
Same 10 requests to every model; a blind AI judge ranked the outputs twice, shuffled (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.59, where 1 is an identical order and 0 unrelated); Plackett-Luce ratings with 95% bootstrap error bars, CLIP and aesthetic scores as checks.
