How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 stickers and icons prompts, written like real users write, sent to 12 models from Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Error bars. Plackett–Luce ratings bootstrapped over requests; CLIP, an aesthetic predictor shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for stickers and icons?
GPT Image 2 (OpenAI): 30% chance of ranking first on 10 stickers and icons requests, ahead of GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst at 18%, out of 12 models tested the same way.
Is GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst as good as GPT Image 2 for stickers and icons?
Close: their 95% error bars overlap (GPT Image 2 +1.27 to +2.24, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +0.63 to +1.99 log-strength), so on 10 requests GPT Image 2 is only the more likely winner.
How was the best model for stickers and icons measured?
Same 10 requests to every model; a blind AI judge ranked the outputs twice, shuffled (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.63, where 1 is an identical order and 0 unrelated); Plackett-Luce ratings with 95% bootstrap error bars, CLIP and aesthetic scores as checks.
