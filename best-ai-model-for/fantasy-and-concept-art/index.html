How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 fantasy and concept art prompts written like real users write them, sent to 12 models you can pick in Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs in shuffled order without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Ratings with error bars. Plackett–Luce fit, bootstrapped over requests. CLIP, an aesthetic predictor are shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for fantasy and concept art?
Nano Banana 2 (Google). On 10 fantasy and concept art requests it had a 29% chance of being ranked first by our judge, ahead of GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst at 16%. 12 Picsart AI models were tested the same way.
Is GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst as good as Nano Banana 2 for fantasy and concept art?
Close. Their 95% error bars overlap (Nano Banana 2 +1.06 to +2.34, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +0.59 to +1.39 log-strength), so on 10 requests we cannot separate them with confidence; Nano Banana 2 is still the more likely winner.
How was the best model for fantasy and concept art measured?
Every model got the same 10 requests. An AI judge ranked the outputs for each request twice in shuffled order without seeing model names (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.52). Rankings were fitted with a Plackett-Luce model and bootstrapped over requests for 95% error bars; CLIP similarity and an aesthetic predictor are supporting scores.
