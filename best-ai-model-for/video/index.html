How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 16 all video tasks prompts, written like real users write, sent to 5 video models from Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's clips without model names, from three frames per clip (at 25, 50 and 75%), then again reshuffled. It does not see motion or hear sound.
  3. Error bars. Plackett–Luce ratings bootstrapped over requests; CLIP and an aesthetic predictor on the same three frames shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for video?
Wan 2.7 (Alibaba): 33% chance of ranking first on 16 video requests, ahead of Seedance 2.5 at 19%, out of 5 video models tested the same way.
Is Seedance 2.5 as good as Wan 2.7 for video?
Close: their 95% error bars overlap (Wan 2.7 +0.04 to +1.12, Seedance 2.5 -0.30 to +0.32 log-strength), so on 16 requests Wan 2.7 is only the more likely winner.
How was the best model for video measured?
Same 16 requests to every model; a blind AI judge ranked the outputs twice, shuffled (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.70, where 1 is an identical order and 0 unrelated); Plackett-Luce ratings with 95% bootstrap error bars, CLIP and aesthetic scores as checks.
