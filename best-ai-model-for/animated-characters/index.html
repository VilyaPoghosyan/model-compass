How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 4 animated character prompts, written like real users write, sent to 5 video models from Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's clips without model names, from three frames per clip (at 25, 50 and 75%), then again reshuffled. It does not see motion or hear sound.
  3. Error bars. Plackett–Luce ratings bootstrapped over requests; CLIP and an aesthetic predictor on the same three frames shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for animated characters?
Veo 3.1 Fast (Google): 30% chance of ranking first on 4 animated characters requests, ahead of Veo 3.1 at 30%, out of 5 video models tested the same way.
Is Veo 3.1 as good as Veo 3.1 Fast for animated characters?
Close: their 95% error bars overlap (Veo 3.1 Fast -1.84 to +2.33, Veo 3.1 -1.83 to +7.11 log-strength), so on 4 requests Veo 3.1 Fast is only the more likely winner.
How was the best model for animated characters measured?
Same 4 requests to every model; a blind AI judge ranked the outputs twice, shuffled (agreement between runs: Kendall tau 0.75, where 1 is an identical order and 0 unrelated); Plackett-Luce ratings with 95% bootstrap error bars, CLIP and aesthetic scores as checks.
