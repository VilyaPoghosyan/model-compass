How this was measured

  1. Same requests. 10 logo with text prompts written like real users write them, sent to 12 models you can pick in Picsart's AI Playground.
  2. Blind judge, twice. An AI judge ranked each request's outputs in shuffled order without model names, then again reshuffled.
  3. Ratings with error bars. Plackett–Luce fit, bootstrapped over requests. CLIP, an aesthetic predictor and OCR are shown as checks.

Questions people ask

Which AI model is best for logo with text?
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (OpenAI). On 10 logo with text requests it had a 27% chance of being ranked first by our judge, ahead of GPT Image 2 at 18%. 12 Picsart AI models were tested the same way.
Is GPT Image 2 as good as GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for logo with text?
Close. Their 95% error bars overlap (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst +0.89 to +2.68, GPT Image 2 +0.32 to +2.16 log-strength), so on 10 requests we cannot separate them with confidence; GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is still the more likely winner.
Which AI model spells the text correctly in logo with text?
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, Luma UNI-1, Ideogram 4.5 and Kling 3.0 Image rendered the requested text exactly on 80% of requests, measured by reading each image back with OCR and comparing it with the words in the prompt.
